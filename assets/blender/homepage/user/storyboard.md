# Nine-chapter visual storyboard

Timeline: 1,440 frames at 24 fps (60 seconds). Markers are editable in Blender's timeline. Camera moves are continuous and reverse naturally when scrubbed backward.

| Chapter | Frame | Visual beat | Current scene cue |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 01 — The First Detail | 1 | Machined shaft and gear detail emerge from a dark studio | Macro start camera, narrow lighting, focus target |
| 02 — The Machine Reveal | 145 | Pull back to the complete elbow assembly | Camera widens into a three-quarter product view |
| 03 — The Exploded Assembly | 300 | Components separate and then return | Components travel on grouped, keyed axial/radial offsets from frames 300–720 |
| 04 — Inside the Gearbox | 445 | Expose both planetary stages | Front-facing housing sector is cut away; ring, sun, planets, carriers and bearings are separate objects |
| 05 — Precision and Manufacturing | 590 | Hold on interfaces and explicit provenance labels | Illustrative text only; no unverified measurements are shown as measured data |
| 06 — Nominal versus Variation | 735 | Introduce a comparison setup | A separately named red display copy exists in the Outliner; it is illustrative geometry, not generated manufacturing variation |
| 07 — The Mechanism in Motion | 880 | Show ideal reduction kinematics | Stage gears and carriers are keyframed at ratios implied by tooth counts; no contact solver is used |
| 08 — A Population of Possible Assemblies | 1050 | Transition toward a population-analysis idea | Story beat and camera move are present; backend population data and charts are intentionally absent |
| 09 — The Final Product Film | 1200 | Return to the assembled reference and close wide | Final camera orbit and composed product shot |

The sequence is a 60-second editable scene scaffold, not a finished automotive-grade film. Preview-render the marked frames and refine them in Blender before any final high-resolution delivery.
