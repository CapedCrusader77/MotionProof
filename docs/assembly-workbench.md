# Assembly workbench

The viewport draws the component meshes served by the backend, posed by backend kinematics.

* Orbit (drag), pan (right-drag), zoom (wheel), Fit.
* Click a component to inspect volume, mass, density, extents, centre of mass, inertia, source.
* Eye icon hides a component; the focus icon isolates it.
* Explode slider, translucent housing (X-ray), section through the assembly centre, joint axes
  (red = driven joint).
* Joint sliders pose the mechanism within the joint ranges.
* Model readiness lists what blocks or weakens an experiment.

Not available: point-to-point measurement in the viewport, arbitrary section planes.
